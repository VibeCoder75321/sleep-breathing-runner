Question 1

Was it a home test or an in-lab sleep study?

This short checker assumes: an adult, a technically adequate study, and events that are mainly obstructive. If the report says the study was inadequate or shows central apneas, complex medical conditions, or another sleep disorder, the pathway is different.
This is an educational tool based on American Academy of Sleep Medicine sleep-testing guidelines. It does not diagnose OSA or UARS.
